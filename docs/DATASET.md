# SPIN XI — what is in the dataset

Every team and season drafted from, derived from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data.
Generated by `npm run archive`; regenerate rather than editing.

**1856 squad-seasons · 34879 player-seasons · 14 competitions**

A squad is everyone who *actually played* for that side that season, so rosters
run from 11 to 37 players and average 18. Players who were signed but never took
the field are absent: the ball-by-ball record cannot see them, and inventing them
would put men in XIs they never belonged to.
