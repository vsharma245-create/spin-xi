## If you only do three things

1. **Post the videos.** Days 1, 5, 12, 19, 25. They are the ones with reach.
2. **Reply to everything** for the first two hours after posting. Every time.
3. **Use the challenge link** whenever you post a record. It is the only link that
   converts, because it gives people something to beat rather than something to read.

## What to watch

Not follower count. Watch:

- how many people open a **challenge link** and finish a season
- how many **daily challenge** entries there are, day over day
- which format got shared, not which got liked

If the daily number is climbing week over week, it is working. If it is flat while
follower count rises, you are collecting an audience that does not play.
