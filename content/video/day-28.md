# Day 28 — Leagues

**Length:** 20s
**Hook (first 2 seconds, on screen):** Settle it over a week, not an evening

## Shot list

**0-5s** — Create a league. Show the code being copied.

**5-10s** — Paste it into a group chat (blur the names).

**10-15s** — Cut to the league table with a few entries in it.

**15-20s** — Top of the table, full screen.

## On-screen text, in order

- One code
- Everyone plays
- Whenever
- Table decides

## Audio

Light and social.

## How to record

Phone: open https://www.spin-xi.com in the browser, screen-record, keep it portrait 9:16.
Desktop: record at 1080×1920 or crop after — never post a landscape recording to Reels.

Keep the first two seconds still and readable. Most people decide there.
