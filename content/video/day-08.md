# Day 8 — Season vs Prime

**Length:** 15s
**Hook (first 2 seconds, on screen):** Same player. Two very different cards.

## Shot list

**0-5s** — Setup: tap SEASON. Draft one recognisable player. Hold on his card and rating.

**5-10s** — Restart, tap PRIME. Draft the same player. Hold on the new rating.

**10-15s** — Split screen or hard cut between the two cards. End on the link.

## On-screen text, in order

- Season form
- ...or career peak
- Same player
- Your call

## Audio

Clean, no vocals — the numbers are the point.

## How to record

Phone: open https://www.spin-xi.com in the browser, screen-record, keep it portrait 9:16.
Desktop: record at 1080×1920 or crop after — never post a landscape recording to Reels.

Keep the first two seconds still and readable. Most people decide there.
