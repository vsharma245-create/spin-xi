# Day 22 — Biggest hit

**Length:** 15s
**Hook (first 2 seconds, on screen):** How far did that go?

## Shot list

**0-4s** — Open a match scorecard. Scroll to the biggest hit / fastest ball panel. Hold.

**4-9s** — Cut to the season records page. Scroll it slowly.

**9-15s** — End on the biggest number of the season, full screen.

## On-screen text, in order

- Biggest hit
- Fastest ball
- Every season keeps its own

## Audio

Impact-heavy. The cut should land on the number.

## How to record

Phone: open https://www.spin-xi.com in the browser, screen-record, keep it portrait 9:16.
Desktop: record at 1080×1920 or crop after — never post a landscape recording to Reels.

Keep the first two seconds still and readable. Most people decide there.
