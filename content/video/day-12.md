# Day 12 — Conditions

**Length:** 18s
**Hook (first 2 seconds, on screen):** Bat or bowl?

## Shot list

**0-5s** — Toss screen. Hold on the condition pills: Day-nighter · Overcast · Dew expected.

**5-9s** — Hover both buttons without tapping. Let the viewer decide first.

**9-13s** — Tap BOWL FIRST. Cut to the match result.

**13-18s** — Scorecard showing the conditions line. End on the link.

## On-screen text, in order

- Heavy sky
- Dew later
- Bat or bowl?
- ...got it right

## Audio

Crowd ambience if you can find it. Otherwise something low and rolling.

## How to record

Phone: open https://www.spin-xi.com in the browser, screen-record, keep it portrait 9:16.
Desktop: record at 1080×1920 or crop after — never post a landscape recording to Reels.

Keep the first two seconds still and readable. Most people decide there.
