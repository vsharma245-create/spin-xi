# Day 5 — Blind draft

**Length:** 25s
**Hook (first 2 seconds, on screen):** Drafting with the ratings hidden

## Shot list

**0-4s** — Setup screen — tap HARD. Show the line: no re-rolls · no restarts · ratings hidden.

**4-16s** — Draft. Pause visibly before two picks. The hesitation is the content.

**16-21s** — XI complete. Ratings now visible — react to what you actually got.

**21-25s** — Result screen. Record on screen. spin-xi.com.

## On-screen text, in order

- Ratings hidden
- No re-rolls
- Just what you know
- ...how did I do?

## Audio

Something tense and quiet, then a drop on the reveal.

## How to record

Phone: open https://www.spin-xi.com in the browser, screen-record, keep it portrait 9:16.
Desktop: record at 1080×1920 or crop after — never post a landscape recording to Reels.

Keep the first two seconds still and readable. Most people decide there.
