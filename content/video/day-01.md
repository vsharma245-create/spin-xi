# Day 1 — What it is

**Length:** 20s
**Hook (first 2 seconds, on screen):** Spin. Draft. Simulate.

## Shot list

**0-3s** — Home screen, tap START DRAFT. Let the reel spin — do not cut it short, the spin is the hook.

**3-8s** — Squad reveals. Hover the best card so the rating is readable.

**8-14s** — Three fast picks. Cut on each tap so it feels quick.

**14-18s** — Cut to the completed XI screen. Hold on the ratings.

**18-20s** — Cut to a result: CHAMPIONS or the record. End on spin-xi.com.

## On-screen text, in order

- Spin for a real squad
- Draft one player
- Eleven times
- Then play the season

## Audio

Whatever is trending and percussive. The taps should land on beat.

## How to record

Phone: open https://www.spin-xi.com in the browser, screen-record, keep it portrait 9:16.
Desktop: record at 1080×1920 or crop after — never post a landscape recording to Reels.

Keep the first two seconds still and readable. Most people decide there.
