# 30 days of content

Everything here is ready to post. You make the accounts and press the button; the
words, the numbers and the shot lists are done.

## What is in here

```
content/
  CALENDAR.md          the month at a glance — what goes out where, and in what format
  x/day-01..30.md      X: caption, format, which asset, when to post
  instagram/day-01..30.md   Instagram: caption, hashtags, carousel slide copy
  video/day-NN.md      shot lists for the ten video days — timings, on-screen text, audio
  reddit/              five posts across the month, not thirty. Reasons below.
  whatsapp/messages.md short lines meant to be pasted into group chats
  images/generate.html open it in a browser, click Download all
```

## The images

Open `images/generate.html` in any browser and press **Download all**. It renders
every card twice — 1200×630 for X, 1080×1080 for Instagram — and saves them to your
downloads folder. Nothing to install.

## The videos

Open `video/generate.html` in Chrome and press **Render & download all**. It animates
six promos on a canvas and saves each one as a real `.webm` video file — 1080×1920,
portrait, ready for Reels, Shorts or X. The animation, the burned-in captions and the
timing are already done. There is nothing to record and nothing to edit.

| Film | Length | Day |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | 20s | 1 |
| Blind draft (Hard mode) | 18s | 5 |
| Season vs Prime | 14s | 8 |
| Live draft | 16s | 19 |
| Speedrun | 22s | 25 |
| What is in here | 16s | 30 |

Chrome or Edge only — Safari cannot record a canvas. To post as MP4, drop the webm
into any online converter, or import it straight into CapCut or InShot; both take webm.

The other four video days (14, 22, 28, 29) have **shot lists** instead, in
`video/day-NN.md`. Those want real footage of the game rather than an animation,
because the whole point of them is the product doing something. Each is a two-minute
screen recording.

Ten of thirty days are video. That is deliberate: it is the only format with real
organic reach left, and a month of still cards is a month of being scrolled past.

### If you would rather use an AI video tool

I have no video model to call, so these are rendered rather than generated. If you
want AI footage on top, the prompts that work for this are literal and short —
"slow push in on a cricket ball on dry turf, late afternoon light, shallow depth of
field, no text" — and you cut it in as B-roll behind the rendered promos. Do not ask
an AI tool to render the interface; it will invent one that is not yours.

## Format mix, and why

| Format | Days | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Video | 10 | The only format with meaningful organic reach left |
| Single card | 7 | Fast to post, good for a stat that lands on its own |
| Carousel | 5 | Instagram's highest-reach static format; people swipe |
| Poll | 5 | Cheapest engagement there is, and it tells you what people care about |
| Text only | 3 | X still rewards a plain take with no image attached |

## Reddit — read this before posting

There are **five** Reddit posts for thirty days, not thirty. Posting daily to
r/Cricket will get the domain banned, not just the account, and then you have lost
the largest cricket community on the internet permanently.

Each post leads with something worth reading and mentions the game once, at the end.
Post as a text post, not a link post. Reply to every comment for the first two hours
— that, and nothing else, decides whether it spreads.

## The one link that converts

On any result screen, share gives you a **challenge link** — it carries the draw you
played, so whoever opens it faces the same eleven spins. A score somebody can answer
is worth far more than a score they can only read.

Use it in replies, in group chats, and any time you post a record.

## Where the numbers came from

Every figure in these posts is read out of the archive: 42,165 player seasons, 9,455
partnerships, ratings built from ball-by-ball data. Nothing is invented, so you can
stand behind all of it if someone in the comments checks.

If you rebuild the archive the numbers may shift slightly. The ones here were true
on the day they were written.

## Timing

- **X** — 7–9pm IST. The largest cricket audience on the platform is Indian evenings.
- **Instagram** — 8–10pm IST for Reels; carousels do fine any time.
- **Reddit** — mid-morning UK time catches both India evening and US morning.
- **WhatsApp** — whenever the group is awake. This is the one that actually spreads.

Do not batch everything to one hour. Spread the day.
