**Subject:** Built a cricket simulator on your ball-by-ball data — thank you

Hello,

I wanted to say thank you properly rather than just use the data quietly.

I've built a free cricket drafting game at https://www.spin-xi.com — you spin for
a real squad-season, draft one player from it, build an eleven and play a
tournament out. Every rating in it is computed from your ball-by-ball records:
15,270 matches, 49,927 rated player-seasons, 2002 to 2026, across Tests, ODIs,
T20Is and eleven domestic leagues. Nothing is copied from any other rating
system.

Two things came out of it that might interest you beyond the game itself.

**Competition strength, measured rather than argued about.** Because hundreds of
players appear in several leagues in the same year, the overlap gives an
exchange rate between them. A two-way model over players and competitions,
solved by alternating, reports the SA20 and the CPL as the hardest places to bat
and the T20 Blast and BPL among the easiest — and the IPL as the hardest place in
the world to bowl. Write-up:
https://www.spin-xi.com/t20-league-strength

**Pace and spin splits per batter.** Classifying every delivery by the bowler's
stated style gives each batter a separate record against each — Gayle in 2018/19
comes out very differently against the two. I hadn't seen that done at
player-season granularity anywhere.

Method is written up in full here, including the three times it was wrong and
how each was caught: https://www.spin-xi.com/how-ratings-work

The data provenance page credits Cricsheet plainly and links to you:
https://www.spin-xi.com/the-archive

No ask attached — I'd have written this anyway. But if you ever keep a list of
things built on the data, I'd be glad to be on it.

Thanks again. It's a genuinely remarkable public resource.
